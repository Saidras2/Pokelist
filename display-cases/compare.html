Assembly, move by move

A
  1. Left wall + front wall → slide the front wall in −X
  2. Right wall + back wall → slide the back wall in +X
  3. Slide the two halves together in −X — both remaining corners mesh at once
  4. Base plate under, magnetic lid on top
B
  1. Drop the front and back walls into the channel
  2. Drop the left and right walls in, butting the corners
  3. Set the lid on — the magnets clamp the stack
C
  1. Stand the front and back walls up, notches facing in
  2. Slide the left wall in +Y — both tabs drop into the notches
  3. Slide the right wall in +Y the same way
  4. Base plate on, magnetic lid on top
Read this before cutting. Acrylic breaks at 2–3% strain and every notch concentrates stress, so a true snap-fit will crack — usually on the second or third assembly. All three of these are press fits, not clicks. Whatever you pick, cut one corner test and tune the finger or tab width to your laser's kerf (typically 0.1–0.2 mm) before committing a full sheet.
