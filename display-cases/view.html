MagnaCase — Pokémon Booster Box Display Case

Acrylic display case with a hidden magnetic lid · 165 × 105 × 140 mm assembled · 5 mm cast acrylic · 4 magnet pairs

01What it looks like

Left: assembled. The 22 mm skirt wraps down over the body and hides the magnets completely — the case reads as a plain acrylic block. Right: lid lifted 45 mm. The red dots are magnets set in the body's outer wall faces; the blue dots are their partners set in the inside of the lid skirt. They land on the same axis, so the skirt gets pulled onto the body in all four directions.

02Cut plan & magnet positions

All nine panels nested on one sheet, drawn at 1:1. The red circles are the eight magnet pockets. Print this at 100% and it is a true-size template you can lay straight onto the acrylic.

05Magnet spec

TypeNeodymium disc, N42
Size6 mm dia × 2 mm
Quantity8 (4 pairs)
Hold each~0.85 kg
Pocket6.2 mm dia × 2 mm deep
Position11 mm below the top rim, centred
A 2 mm-deep pocket leaves 3 mm of acrylic behind the magnet. A 3 mm-thick magnet would leave only 2 mm, which is where acrylic starts star-cracking under load — so if you want thicker magnets, step the walls up to 6 mm.

Body walls get their pocket in the outer face; lid skirt strips get theirs in the inner face. Drill blind, never through.

06Build order

  1. Drill the eight magnet pockets first, while the panels are still flat. Brad-point bit, ~500 rpm, sacrificial backer board, tape on the bit to mark 2 mm depth.
  2. Dry-fit everything with masking tape. Check both diagonals are equal.
  3. Weld the body: bottom → side walls → front/back walls, with Weld-On® 4 by capillary action (25 G needle and syringe). Don't flood it.
  4. Weld the lid skirt into a ring first, let it cure, then weld the ring to the lid top.
  5. Glue the four body magnets in. Then slide the lid on and let each lid magnet find its own orientation — the body magnet pulls it in the right way round. Glue it there. That is the whole polarity problem solved.
  6. Cure 24–48 h. Add felt pads to the base, flame-polish the exposed top edges.
Before you cut anything: booster box sizes drift between sets, and the 140 × 80 × 125 mm envelope above is a typical hobby figure, not a manufacturer spec. Measure your actual box, then edit BOX_W, BOX_D and BOX_H at the top of openscad/booster_box_case.scad. Full detail is in SPEC.md.
